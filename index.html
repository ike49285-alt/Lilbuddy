A kilometre of valley, half a kilometre wide, tilted gently toward the sea. A pump at the top sends the river in, more in the spring melt and after storms upstream, with the sand it carries. The water moves as a sheet, the sand moves where the water drags hard enough at it, and the river shapes its own channel: cutting the outside of its bends, building bars on the inside, breaking its banks in a flood and building a delta into the sea. Plants grow on dry ground and hold the banks together. The clock runs on the river bed's time: at the faster rates the bed's changes are sped up, as river models do.
